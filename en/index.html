FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Q. What is InnoLive?

InnoLive is a broadcast tool that de-identifies other people's faces in real time during a live stream. Register people who are allowed to appear, and everyone else is covered automatically.

Q. The app does not work on my device.

InnoLive requires iOS 26 or later and Android 11 or later for stable operation.

Q. Which streaming platforms can I use?

InnoLive currently supports YouTube and CHZZK.

Q. My face is covered on the stream.

To appear on the broadcast, register your own face as an allowed person first. If you already did, make sure your face is clearly visible to the camera.

Q. Can I register more than one face?

Yes. You can register each co-host as an allowed person. Free allows up to 2 faces, and Streamer and above allow up to 5.
